# Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the profitability and margin performance of different product lines for Nassau Candy Distributor.

The analysis was performed using Python and data analytics techniques to identify high-performing products, product divisions, regional profitability, gross margins, costs, and profit efficiency.

The project provides business insights into which products and divisions contribute significantly to overall gross profit and where profitability opportunities exist.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze sales, cost, and gross profit performance
- Evaluate gross margin across product divisions
- Identify high-profit and high-margin products
- Compare profitability across regions
- Analyze cost and profit efficiency
- Perform Pareto analysis of product profitability
- Generate actionable business insights

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Product Profitability
Analyzed total sales, units, gross profit, gross margin, and profit per unit for individual products.

### Division Analysis
Compared Chocolate, Other, and Sugar divisions based on sales, cost, gross profit, and gross margin.

### Regional Analysis
Evaluated profitability across different business regions.

### Cost & Profitability Efficiency
Analyzed overall sales, cost, gross profit, gross margin, cost per unit, and profit per unit.

### Pareto Analysis
The analysis found that the top 4 products contribute approximately **77.30% of total gross profit**, indicating that a small number of products generate a significant share of overall profitability.

## 📈 Key Results

- **Total Sales:** ₹141,783.63
- **Total Cost:** ₹48,340.83
- **Total Gross Profit:** ₹93,442.80
- **Overall Gross Margin:** 65.91%
- **Overall Profit per Unit:** ₹2.42
- **Top 4 Products' Gross Profit Contribution:** 77.30%

## 💡 Business Insights

- A small group of products contributes a significant portion of total gross profit.
- Product profitability varies considerably across products and divisions.
- Gross margin analysis helps identify products with stronger profitability.
- Regional analysis highlights differences in business performance.
- Cost and profit-per-unit analysis provides additional insight into operational efficiency.
