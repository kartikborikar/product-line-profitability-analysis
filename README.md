# Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the profitability and margin performance of different product lines for Nassau Candy Distributor.

The analysis was performed using Python and data analytics techniques to evaluate product profitability, division performance, regional performance, gross margins, costs, and profit efficiency.

The project provides business insights into which products and divisions contribute significantly to overall gross profit and identifies areas of profitability opportunity.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales, cost, and gross profit performance
* Evaluate gross margin across product divisions
* Identify high-profit and high-margin products
* Compare profitability across regions
* Analyze cost and profit efficiency
* Perform Pareto analysis of product profitability
* Generate actionable business insights

---

---

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Product Profitability

Analyzed total sales, units, gross profit, gross margin, and profit per unit for individual products.

### Division Analysis

Compared Chocolate, Other, and Sugar divisions based on sales, cost, gross profit, and gross margin.

### Regional Analysis

Evaluated sales, gross profit, gross margin, and profitability across different business regions.

### Cost & Profitability Efficiency

Analyzed overall sales, cost, gross profit, gross margin, cost per unit, and profit per unit.

### Pareto Analysis

Pareto analysis was performed to identify the products contributing the largest share of total gross profit.

The analysis found that the **top 4 products contribute approximately 77.30% of total gross profit**, showing that a relatively small group of products accounts for a substantial share of overall profitability.

---

## 📈 Key Results

| Metric                                        |      Result |

## 💡 Business Insights

* A small group of products contributes a significant portion of total gross profit.
* Product profitability varies considerably across products and divisions.
* Gross margin analysis helps identify products with stronger profitability.
* Regional analysis highlights differences in business performance.
* Profit-per-unit analysis provides additional insight into product-level efficiency.
* Cost analysis helps understand the relationship between operational costs and profitability.
